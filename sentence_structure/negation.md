# Negation (Negasjon)

The primary negation word in Norwegian is **"ikke"** (not). Its position differs between
main clauses and subordinate clauses — this is one of the most important word order rules.

## Notation
```
<negert_setning> ::= <negert_hoved_setning> | <negert_bi_setning>

<negert_hoved_setning> ::= <subjekt> <verbal> "ikke" <resten>
                         | <forfelt> <verbal> <subjekt> "ikke" <resten>

<negert_bi_setning> ::= <subjunksjon> <subjekt> "ikke" <verbal> <resten>

<resten> ::= <objekt> | <adverbial> | <predikativ> | ε

<subjunksjon> ::= "fordi" | "når" | "hvis" | "at" | "selv om" | ...
```

## Key rule

| Clause type        | Position of "ikke"              |
|--------------------|----------------------------------|
| Main clause        | **after** the finite verb        |
| Subordinate clause | **before** the finite verb       |

## Examples

### Main clause negation (ikke after verb)
| Norsk                          | Engelsk                        |
|-------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Jeg spiser **ikke** fisk.     | I do not eat fish.             |
| Hun er **ikke** hjemme.       | She is not home.               |
| Vi kan **ikke** komme.        | We cannot come.                |
| I dag spiser jeg **ikke** fisk. | Today I am not eating fish.  |

### Subordinate clause negation (ikke before verb)
| Norsk                                          | Engelsk                              |
|------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Han sier at han **ikke** spiser fisk.          | He says that he does not eat fish.   |
| Jeg vet ikke hvorfor hun **ikke** er hjemme.  | I don't know why she is not home.    |
| Hvis du **ikke** kommer, ringer jeg.          | If you don't come, I will call.      |
| Fordi vi **ikke** kan komme, sender vi brev.  | Because we cannot come, we send a letter. |

### Negation with modal verbs
Modal + infinitive: "ikke" goes between the modal and the infinitive in main clauses,
but before the modal in subordinate clauses.

| Clause      | Norsk                            | Engelsk                     |
|-------------|----------------------------------|-----------------------------|
| Main        | Jeg vil **ikke** reise.         | I do not want to travel.    |
| Subordinate | ... at jeg **ikke** vil reise.  | ... that I don't want to travel. |

## Other negation words
| Norsk   | Engelsk      | Note                              |
|---------|--------------|-----------------------------------|
| ikke    | not          | general negation                  |
| aldri   | never        | same position rules as "ikke"     |
| ingen   | no one / none | replaces a noun phrase           |
| ingenting | nothing    | replaces an object noun phrase    |
| hverken...eller | neither...nor | correlative conjunction |
