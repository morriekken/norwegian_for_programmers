# Irregular verbs (Uregelmessige verb / Sterke verb)

Irregular verbs do not follow the v1 or v2 conjugation patterns. Their stems change unpredictably between forms and must be memorised individually.

## Notation
```
<uregelmessig_verbbøyning> ::= <infinitiv> | <presens> | <preteritum> | <perfektum_partisipp>

<infinitiv>           ::= "å" <stamme_inf>
<presens>             ::= <stamme_presens>
<preteritum>          ::= <stamme_pret>
<perfektum_partisipp> ::= <stamme_part>
```

Note: unlike regular verbs, each stem form (`<stamme_inf>`, `<stamme_presens>`, `<stamme_pret>`, `<stamme_part>`) must be looked up individually — they cannot be derived by a suffix rule.

The full present perfect tense is: `"har" <perfektum_partisipp>`

## Examples by pattern

### Completely irregular (must memorise)
| Infinitiv | Presens | Preteritum | Pres. perfektum | Engelsk     |
|-----------|---------|------------|-----------------|-------------|
| ha        | har     | hadde      | har hatt        | have        |
| være      | er      | var        | har vært        | be          |
| gjøre     | gjør    | gjorde     | har gjort       | do          |
| vite      | vet     | visste     | har visst       | know        |
| si        | sier    | sa         | har sagt        | say         |
